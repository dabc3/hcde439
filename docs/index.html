I have some experience in physical computing from high school but I am excited to shake off the cobwebs and learn some new skills in this class. I am eager to bring physical components of prototyping to life in this class and translate my coding skills to physical computing.

Assignment 1: Blink!

I made a blinking Christmas light pattern using three LEDs and calculated the resistors needed for each LED.

Assignment 2: Fade!

I made a fading set of LEDs that turn on and off using a button switch three LEDs and calculated the resistors needed for each LED.

Assignment 3: Input Output!

I made a pair of alternating leds that turn on and off based on the light taken in by the photoresistor.

Assignment 4: Libraries!

I made a servo that only moves when the light sensor reads high and the temperature sensor reads high.

Assignment 5: High Voltages and Transistors!

I made a series of white leds that fade when button 1 is hit on the IR remote, and turn off when button 2 is hit.
